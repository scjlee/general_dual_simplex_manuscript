Fig 1F. Decreased SNR is associated with poor spectral separation by singular value decomposition.




  (F) Except under multiplicative noise, perturbation drastically narrows the gap between σ3 and σ4. Lower post-scaling SNR is associated
  with weaker spectral separation. Left: mean normalized singular values across 20 simulations, with shading showing the full range, at noise
  level 0.25. Center: singular-value ratios across noise levels. Right: post-scaling SNR versus singular-value ratio.
